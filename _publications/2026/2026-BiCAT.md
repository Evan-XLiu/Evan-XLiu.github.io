abstract: >-
  TL;DR: BiCAT is an action tokenizer for bimanual VLA models, which models arm coordination within the tokens by sharing one motion representation across arms and conditioning each arm’s tokens on its partner.
abstract_zh: >-
  TL;DR：BiCAT 是一种面向双臂 VLA 模型的动作分词器。它让双臂共享同一套运动表征，并在生成每条机械臂的 token 时以另一条臂为条件，从而在 token 中建模双臂协调。
