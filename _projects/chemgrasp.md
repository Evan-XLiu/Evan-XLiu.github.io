## Overview

ChemGrasp combines task-specific grasp optimization and motion planning to manipulate closed chemical vessels safely and dexterously.

## Why it matters

Laboratory objects impose safety constraints that are not captured by generic grasping objectives. ChemGrasp incorporates task affordances into grasp selection and execution.
