## Overview

ContactExplorer encourages a dexterous hand to discover diverse, useful interaction patterns by explicitly tracking state-conditioned finger–object contact coverage.

## Why it matters

Exploration in dexterous manipulation is difficult because meaningful contacts are sparse and highly dependent on the current object state. This project studies contact itself as a structured exploration signal.

## Contribution

I contributed to this collaborative research project and its evaluation for general-purpose dexterous manipulation. More technical details, results, and videos are available on the project website.
