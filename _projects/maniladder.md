## Overview

ManiLadder organizes diverse robot manipulation tasks into a structured and difficulty-calibrated benchmark.

## Why it matters

Robot learning results are often difficult to compare across embodiments, tasks, and datasets. A common task ladder makes progress more measurable and helps expose where methods succeed or fail.

## Contribution

I participated in the collaborative development and evaluation of the benchmark. The project website will contain the latest task definitions, results, and resources.
