## Overview

ManiLadder covers single-arm and dual-arm manipulation of rigid and deformable objects using grippers or dexterous hands. It calibrates task difficulty using the success rate of a reference policy trained and evaluated under the same protocol for every task.

## Why it matters

Robot learning results are often difficult to compare across embodiments, tasks, and datasets. A common task ladder makes progress more measurable and helps expose where methods succeed or fail.
